A newspaper front page summing up the world news of Sunday 22 November 2026 in a single 1024×1024 image. The poster is drawn as original SVG artwork and rendered to `entry.png` by `render.mjs` (HTML + headless Chromium); `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies it to the output directory. Two runs write identical bytes.

## The six stories

To rebuild the poster from scratch: `node render.mjs` (writes `entry.png`), then `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`.

1. **Mexico seizes 'El Chapo Isidro', one of the FBI's most wanted** — Mexican troops captured Fausto Isidro Meza Flores in a night raid in Sinaloa, along with two other cartel leaders; a $5m FBI reward was on his head. (Reuters / BBC)
2. **COP31 nears a deal as talks run into Sunday** — negotiators worked past their Friday deadline into Sunday; a new package goes to nearly 200 nations for approval on Sunday morning in Antalya. (Reuters)
3. **Russian ship ablaze in the Baltic after drone strike** — the sanctioned cargo ship Sparta II was hit by drones in the Gulf of Finland; its 16 crew escaped as the fire burned on and Finland led the rescue. (Reuters / CNN)
4. **Bahrain hit as Iran war widens** — an Iranian attack set an industrial facility ablaze while Bahrain's air defences intercepted other strikes; home to the US Navy's Fifth Fleet, it warned residents of a possible new threat. (Reuters / Al Jazeera)
5. **FBI's crime database is breached** — hackers broke into the nationwide crime database used for warrants and background checks; the second FBI breach in months. (CNN)
6. **Alcaraz faces Zverev in ATP final** — Carlos Alcaraz fought back past Ben Shelton to set up Sunday's final in Turin against world No. 1 Alexander Zverev, who seeks a third title; Alcaraz seeks his first. (BBC)

All artwork is original SVG. No photographs, no copied art, no news-site logos.
